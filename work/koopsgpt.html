AI Work / Case Study

KoopsGPT: an LLM suite a manufacturer actually uses

A custom agentic LLM suite I proposed, built, and shipped at Koops Automation Systems. It runs in production for 40+ employees.

40+ daily users 4,000+ hours/year saved

Context

Quoting, contract review, and risk analysis at an automation builder meant hours inside dense documents and tribal knowledge. I was an intern; I pitched leadership on building this and then built it.

What I built

KoopsGPT wires LLM APIs and retrieval (RAG) over Koops's enterprise data so the BD team can quote faster, review contracts against past precedent, and flag risk earlier. Adoption was designed in from day one: I trained the team, gathered feedback in person, and shipped what they asked for next.

Outcome

40+ daily users saving 2+ hours per user per week, roughly 4,000+ hours a year. It outlived my internship, which is the outcome I am proudest of.

What I learned

Adoption was the real product. Sitting with the team, watching where they hesitated, and shipping the next thing they actually asked for did more for usage than any model upgrade.
