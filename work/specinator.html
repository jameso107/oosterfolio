AI Work / Case Study

SPECINATOR: reading specs so engineers don't have to

An AI decision-support tool I built through SYZYGY for an electrical engineering firm's business development team.

15 min to 4 min per document 92% to 98% accuracy

Context

Every inbound opportunity meant manually reading long specification documents to decide fit. Intake took about 15 minutes per document at 92% accuracy, and the misses cost real bids.

What I built

A pipeline that ingests each document, extracts the decision-relevant requirements, and presents a structured recommendation with the source passages attached, so the BD team verifies instead of hunts. Under the hood it is prompt design plus retrieval (RAG) over the firm's specification library. Humans stay in the loop on every accept/reject decision.

How I tested it

Before each release I scored outputs with LLM-as-judge evaluation and traced failures back through the pipeline to their root cause, then re-ran until the failure mode was closed.

Outcome

Intake time dropped from 15 to 4 minutes per document. Accuracy rose from 92% to 98%. The business outcome: the team screens every inbound opportunity instead of triaging which documents get read.

What I learned

Accuracy numbers alone did not win the team over. Showing the source passages next to every recommendation did, because it turned "trust the tool" into "check the tool's work in seconds."
