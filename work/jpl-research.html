AI Work / Research

Do experts trust AI? Field research inside Mars operations

At NASA JPL I ran an embedded ethnographic study of how Mars Science Laboratory (Curiosity rover) operators actually use Faro, a GenAI analysis tool, during downlink operations.

67% expert/novice trust gap NASA Shining Star Award (4 of 1,200+)

What I did

I sat with expert and novice mission operators through real operations, instrumented the study with a gaze tracking tool I built on OpenFace 2.0 (mapping gaze to on-screen regions to produce heatmaps and deterministic measurements of tool utilization), and combined the qualitative and quantitative records.

What I found

A statistically significant mistrust of Faro among expert operators: a 67% difference in trust between experts and novices. New operators leaned on the tool; the people with the most experience trusted it least.

What happened next

I delivered a 5-point recommendation plan to prepare Faro for mission-critical use, and JPL recognized the work with the NASA Shining Star Award, given to 4 interns out of 1,200+ across all NASA centers.

Why it matters for building

Every AI product I ship now starts from this finding: adoption is a trust problem before it is a model problem, and human-in-the-loop design is how you earn it.
